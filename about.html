our story — est. 2021

Made in the darkroom,
born from a broken scanner.

✦ ✦ ✦

In 2021, a secondhand flatbed scanner chewed through a box of family photographs. Most came out streaked, burned, haloed with red light. One came out better than the original.

That accident — halation, the glow that blooms when light pierces film and bounces back — became an obsession. Why did the flaw feel more honest than the photograph?

We spent three years bottling that answer: filters tuned by hand, tested on hundreds of frames each, named after the eras they remember. No AI slop, no preset packs resold. Just chemistry, patience, and respect for the image.

Today, 192,000 pictures have been reborn here. Every one of them stayed on its owner's device. That's not a feature — it's the whole point.
